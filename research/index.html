WHAT THE ARTISTS ACTUALLY DID

Different minds.
Different instruments.

Territory’s Age of Ultron case study describes different research and visual identities for Stark’s engineering, Banner’s biology and Cho’s bioengineering. That is the useful lesson for this portfolio: each project should look like the subject it helps someone understand.

Sony Imageworks documents geometry-based linework, halftoning, hatching, print-inspired registration, stepped animation and drawn effects. Here, those ideas become readable edges, meaningful marks and brief response accents. Pointer input and scientific time remain continuous.

Territory also used photographed organic material and optical experimentation in Blade Runner 2049. A texture can be beautiful without being a scientific measurement; our labels keep that distinction visible.

THE OPEN TECHNOLOGY / WHAT IT REALLY ENABLES

Five folders, with a real path forward.

Form & force

Can the structure find its own shape?

General inverse form-finding: specify several geometric and force goals, optimize them with JAX FDM, then use Manifold to turn a selected design into inspectable solid geometry.

Open this folder
JAX FDMMIT · Python / JAX

Differentiable equilibrium and inverse design of pin-jointed networks.

Researched future engine; not integrated. Structural certification is outside scope.

ManifoldApache-2.0 · browser WASM

Robust solid geometry, boolean cuts and model export.

A geometry kernel, not a stress solver. Not integrated yet.

Motion & mechanisms

What makes a thing move with purpose?

Teach an original articulated mechanism to follow a drawn path. Use real contact, joint limits and measured tracking error; choreograph the motion without hiding failed plans.

Open this folder
MuJoCo / official WASMApache-2.0 source · browser WASM

Google DeepMind’s articulated dynamics engine now has maintained JavaScript bindings.

Bindings are still work in progress. They are researched, not installed here; planning and control still require implementation.

Openmovement OM10CC BY-SA 3.0 documentation

Published tooth counts, frequency and inertia ground the current watch study.

Original schematic geometry; not Rolex or Omega manufacturer CAD.

Light, sound & patterns

What could we understand if we could see it?

Light written by matter: draw a target image and optimize a simulated glass surface that projects it. A separate molecular study could inspect real structures and their provenance.

Open this folder
Mitsuba / inverse causticsFree research renderer · BSD-style terms

Optimizes a glass heightfield to produce a target image through simulated light.

Requires a local/server Python/C++ job. It is not a browser shader trick or an implemented feature here.

Mol*MIT · browser WebGL

Real macromolecular structure inspection, selections and saved views.

A researched biology path. Molecular visualization does not predict folding or validate biological function.

Earth & place

How does technology learn to care for a place?

A replayable place study: compare changes in a coastal dataset through time, preserving coverage gaps, source revisions and uncertainty as visible layers.

Open this folder
VTK.jsBSD-3-Clause · browser

Scientific geometry and scalar-volume visualization, clipping and data readers.

Rendering support is not an environmental model. New datasets need coverage, provenance and unit checks.

Wikidata data accessStructured data CC0

Sourced identifiers and coordinates for an open place atlas.

Current atlas already uses Wikidata. External media and CAD keep their own licenses.

Software & agency

Can we rehearse a decision before trusting it?

Turn real traces and declared system constraints into diagrams that stay readable while you change them. Keep AI proposals separate from the checks that accept them.

Open this folder
Penrose / BloomMIT · browser / WASM

Constraint-based technical diagrams and interactive optimization.

A diagram optimizer, not a physical solver or proof that an architecture is correct. Researched, not integrated yet.

OpenTelemetry OTLPOpen specification

Recorded operation timing and parent relationships for inspectable software.

WEAVE already reads bounded local OTLP files. Recorded duration alone does not establish causality.

Use the right engine for the question.

The portfolio remains directly usable in a browser. MuJoCo’s official WASM bindings, Manifold and Mol* offer credible browser paths for future instruments. JAX FDM and Mitsuba need a different execution path: a local or hosted scientific job, with actual progress, cancellation and downloadable results.

Unreal can provide a separate spatial presentation once a model and interaction have earned it. It does not replace an equilibrium solver, a dataset’s provenance or a useful task. No new native Unreal scene or remotely streamed engine is claimed by this rebuild.
