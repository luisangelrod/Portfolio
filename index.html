How I work

The tools change. The discipline does not.

Understand before touching

In an existing codebase I read the data model first, then trace one real request end to end so I learn the conventions before adding to them.

Evidence over confidence

Builds, tests, and observed behavior decide whether work is done. On my last payment platform that meant hardware verification gates before every release.

Modern tools, full accountability

I use coding assistants daily and treat their output as an untrusted contribution until review and tests confirm it. I remain able to explain and debug every line I ship.

Communicate like a contractor

Years of government and enterprise contract work taught me to scope clearly, surface risk early, and deliver without hand holding.
