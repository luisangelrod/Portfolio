WHAT’S HAPPENING HERE

Observation.
Prediction. Difference.

Samples are generated from stated models. A projectile follows constant acceleration; the pendulum uses a small-angle approximation with a 2 m length; the bouncing ball uses a restitution of 0.72. Air resistance is omitted.

For your own clip, mark a known distance and track at least four positions at different times. The tool fits horizontal constant velocity and vertical constant acceleration. The residual reports disagreement with that model. Perspective, camera movement, occlusion, and tracking errors can all affect the result.

Automatic tracking compares a small image patch between frames. It can lose the object; inspect the dots and correct them manually. The app does not infer mass or unseen depth.
