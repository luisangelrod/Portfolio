How this is calculated

Radial gap = (case diameter − 2 × wall − 26) / 2. Axial gap = case height − crystal − 0.8 back − 3.9 movement − 0.4 dial − 1.4 hand envelope, in mm.

Only the 26 × 3.9 mm movement envelope is documented. Other dimensions are design assumptions. This checks simple cylindrical overlap, not assembly compatibility, tolerances or water resistance.
