The person behind the project

A note from Luis.

Physics, geometry, nature and space are connected parts of my curiosity. This bench turns those interests into operable models: a tension network, an emergent pattern field and central-gravity trajectories. Drawing and color expose quantities; the model notes explain their limits.

Who it helps

Curious people who want to change a condition and understand its consequence.
