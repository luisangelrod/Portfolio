LOOK UNDER THE RESULT

Every change leaves a thread.

The reference uses the open OM10’s documented 3.5 Hz frequency and 18 mg·cm² balance inertia. This experiment adds an ideal torsional oscillator and couples its rate to the illustrated gear train.

f = 3.5 √(stiffness / inertia)ratios relative to the reference
Sources, equation & model limits

The implied reference stiffness is 8.7050e-7 N·m/rad, derived from the ideal equation Iθ″ + κθ = 0. It is not a measured spring specification. The graphical amplitude and layout are illustrative. The comparison excludes friction, escapement contact, temperature and wear, and does not predict a real watch’s accuracy.

Gold marks the selected connection. Cyan is the reference; coral is the alternative. Traveling points trace a relationship; they do not measure energy. Separation and section cuts change the display only.
