THE CONNECTION IS VISIBLE

Useful information.
An ordinary screen.

Your small fieldbook is encoded into numbered QR frames. The receiver collects them in any order, ignores duplicates, and verifies a SHA-256 checksum before showing an import preview. Different versions of the same note remain visible for you to resolve.

This prototype uses a repeating sequence of frames, with a 16 KB transfer limit. Both devices must have the app available before disconnecting. This is a nearby, intentional transfer; it does not send messages to distant or absent people.

Camera access works on this computer’s localhost. A separate phone needs a trusted HTTPS address on that phone. Image and transfer-file imports provide an alternative. Browser storage can be cleared, so export a backup of anything you want to keep.
