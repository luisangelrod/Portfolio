A small accounting instrument

Where every
cent goes.

0% is supported. Up to 600 months; no fees or extra payments.

Fixed nominal annual interest, monthly accrual. Interest rounds half-up each month. At 0%, remainder cents are distributed across installments; otherwise the final payment settles the remaining balance.

Then

The original formula divided by zero at 0% and used absolute values to hide a negative remaining balance.

Now

Integer-cent accounting, explicit monthly rounding, a separate 0% allocation, and an exact final payoff.

The original repositories stay intact. These local editions make the assumptions visible and the edge cases testable.
